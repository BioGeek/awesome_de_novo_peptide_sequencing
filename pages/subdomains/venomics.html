Workflows (38)

  • Wolf spider venom AMPs (lycocitins) (2004): De novo sequences the antimicrobial peptides lycocitin 1, 2 and 3 from the venom glands of the wolf spider Lycosa singoriensis, two of them new, alongside an unassigned 3038.70 Da peptide.
  • Atractaspis irregularis sarafotoxins (2005): Direct FTMS analysis of crude Atractaspis irregularis venom, with de novo sequencing confirming two new sarafotoxin families and characterizing a third, combined with precursor cloning.
  • Pseudonaja textilis venom diversity (2006): Profiles the venom of the Australian eastern brown snake by 2-DE, MS and de novo sequencing, finding new venom proteins and their modifications.
  • Conus virgo T-superfamily conotoxins (2006): Direct FTMS analysis of crude Conus virgo venom with disulfide reduction allowed de novo sequencing of two new modified T-superfamily conotoxins.
  • Bromotryptophan conotoxin sequencing (2006): Sequences a T-1-family conotoxin de novo by FTICR and maps its disulfides, a class whose 10 to 17 residues carry unusual modifications including brominated tryptophan that defeat database identification.
  • Australian elapid snake venom proteomes (2007): 2-DE and de novo peptide sequencing catalogue venom proteins of 18 Australian elapid species, including several novel protein families.
  • Cane toad parotid secretion peptides (2011): De novo sequencing of peptides in the parotid secretion of the cane toad Bufo marinus, a highly toxic secretion whose peptide content, unlike amphibian skin, had gone unexamined.
  • Micrurus tener toxin partial de novo sequencing (2012): Manual and automated de novo sequencing with CID and ETD data recovered the N-terminal half of a disulfide-bridged Texas coral snake toxin.
  • Araneus ventricosus venomics (2013): Manual de novo sequencing combined with a cDNA-derived local database identified 130 toxin-like proteins in a spider venom and found methyl-esterified glutamate.
  • Tityus serrulatus venom PTM toxins (2013): Characterises Tityus serrulatus scorpion venom, including phospho- and glycoproteome, combining database search with de novo sequencing to reveal modified toxins.
  • Spider venom peptidome (SPITC + de novo) (2014): SPITC N-terminal derivatization + 18O labeling coupled with de novo peptide sequencing to simultaneously identify and quantify spider-venom peptides from species with unknown genomes.
  • Indonesian king cobra top-down venomics (2015): Top-down and bottom-up venomics of Ophiophagus hannah, where de novo sequence tags from intact toxins identified three-finger toxins, a novel PLA2 and other proteins.
  • Bothriopsis taeniata PLA2 K49 (2015): Automatic de novo sequencing (PEAKS Studio) with CID/HCD/ETD triplex MS/MS to elucidate the full-length sequence of a new PLA2 K49 (Btt-TX) from Bothriopsis taeniata snake venom.
  • Cone snail contryphans (2017): De novo sequencing of twelve short single-disulfide conopeptides from four cone snail species, including the shortest contryphan characterised and one with a brominated tryptophan.
  • Loxosceles intermedia venom proteome (2017): Multi-protease, multi-dissociation proteomics of brown spider venom, with spectral networks and meta-contig assembly yielding de novo sequences of nearly complete proteins.
  • Scorpion venom AMP sequencing (2017): Completes the sequences of antimicrobial peptides in Isometrus maculatus scorpion venom by mass spectrometry, where venom-gland transcriptomics had multiplied candidate AMPs without confirming the mature peptides.
  • Russell’s viper apyrase (Ruviapyrase) (2018): Purifies a novel apyrase from Daboia russelii venom whose identity rested on de novo sequences carrying apyrase domains.
  • Bufo gargarizans skin peptidome (2018): Peptidomics workflow using nano-LC-MS/MS and PEAKS de novo sequencing against a transcriptome-derived database to characterise <10 kDa bioactive peptides from Bufo gargarizans (Venenum Bufonis) skin secretions.
  • Hypnale hypnale venomics (2018): Venomics workflow combining SDS-PAGE + LC-MS/MS with Novor + PEAKS de novo sequencing and MS-BLAST similarity search to characterize the venom proteome of the hump-nosed pit viper (Western Ghats).
  • Ruby ant venom decapeptide (2019): Combined Edman degradation and de novo peptide sequencing to isolate and characterize U-MYRTX-MRArub1, a novel insecticidal decapeptide from Myrmica rubra ant venom.
  • Scorpion venom chemical-modification de novo (2019): Chemical modification-assisted de novo sequencing of antimicrobial peptides in scorpion venom.
  • Duttaphrynus melanostictus parotoid secretion proteins (2019): Proteins of the Asian common toad parotoid macrogland secretion were identified by database search and de novo analysis, the latter yielding 153 peptides.
  • Gyroxin structural variants (2019): De novo peptide sequencing showing structural variants of the gyroxin toxin in the venom of the rattlesnake Crotalus durissus terrificus.
  • Conus amadis conotoxin discovery (2019): Proteome-based de novo sequencing of novel conotoxins from the molluscivorous cone snail Conus amadis, with the neurological activity of the natural venom assayed in zebrafish.
  • Malabar pit viper venomics (2020): Venomics workflow combining SDS-PAGE and ion-exchange pre-fractionation with LC-MS/MS + Novor and PEAKS de novo sequencing to identify 97 proteins (including 18 exclusive to de novo) from Trimeresurus malabaricus venom, and evaluate antivenom cross-reactivity.
  • Anatolian meadow viper top-down ISD venomics (2020): Extended top-down venomics workflow using in-source decay (ISD) with 1,5-diaminonaphthalene reducing matrix to enable disulfide-bond counting and de novo sequencing-based identification of high-molecular-weight venom constituents, applied to the newly described Vipera anatolica senliki.
  • Conus inscriptus conotoxins (2021): Manual de novo sequencing of eleven novel conotoxins from the venom of the vermivorous cone snail Conus inscriptus.
  • Buthacus leptochelys venom linear peptide (2021): De novo structure determination of a linear, disulfide-free peptide from the venom of the scorpion Buthacus leptochelys.
  • Naja naja venomics (2022): Venomics + antivenomics workflow using SDS-PAGE, RP-HPLC and Q-TOF LC-MS/MS with PEAKS + Novor de novo sequencing to identify 115 proteins across 17 venom protein families in Indian cobra venom from the Western Ghats, and benchmark polyvalent-antivenom recognition.
  • Hydrophis schistosus venomics (2022): Combined bottom-up proteomics with a transcriptome database and hybrid de novo sequencing to characterize beaked sea snake venom, the de novo step adding 46 toxin peptides.
  • Daboia siamensis multi-omics venomics (2022): Multi-omics venomics of Russell’s viper (Daboia siamensis, Thai specimens): 10x-linked-read + short-read genome assembly and venom-gland transcriptomics feed a MAKER-annotated CDS database, which is searched with PEAKS Studio (database) plus SMSNet (deep-learning de novo) to identify 1,662 venom proteins including previously unreported hyaluronidase-1, phospholipase B and waprin.
  • Bothrops moojeni venom protease de novo identification (2022): Reviews the state of identifying wholly unknown proteins by mass spectrometry and demonstrates it on a snake venom serine protease from Bothrops moojeni, where de novo sequence tags such as D[K/Q]D[I/L]VDD[K/Q] made the identification.
  • Batroxin I bradykinin-potentiating peptide (2023): Isolates a cytotoxic bradykinin-potentiating peptide from Bothrops atrox venom and determines its sequence by de novo MS/MS.
  • Snake venomics quantification review (2023): Calvete-lab review of mass-spectrometry-based quantification of snake venom proteomes for non-model species: label-free strategies, absolute quantification via ICP-MS, hybrid elemental/molecular MS setups. Discusses manual de novo interpretation as an established workflow for non-model venomics.
  • MELD snake venomics (2023): Applies multi-enzymatic limited digestion to four snake venoms, increasing de novo sequenced peptides and toxin coverage in PEAKS.
  • Scorpion venom short peptides (2024): LC-HRMS with de novo sequencing of short (2-12 aa) scorpion venom peptides: 156 sequences identified, revealing N- and C-terminal modifications including the previously unreported N-benzoyl modification.
  • Spiderhawk wasp venom oligopeptides (2024): De novo sequencing of Pepsis decorata wasp venom identified 39 oligopeptides, including proctolin-like and bradykinin-related peptides.
  • Conus regius conopeptidome (2025): Reversed-phase LC + Q-TOF MS/MS with Peaks Studio v7 de novo sequencing to identify 520 conopeptides from the vermivorous cone snail Conus regius, spanning 13 conotoxin gene superfamilies and eight disulfide-poor peptide classes: the first molluscan entry in the venomics lane.

Workflows (18)

  • Melanoma HLA peptidome immunogenicity (2012): HLA class I peptidomes of four melanoma cell lines, extracted from isolated HLA, separated by two-dimensional HPLC and sequenced by MALDI post-source-decay MS. Spectra were interpreted twice over, by database-dependent Mascot search and by database-independent de novo sequencing in Sequit!, with synthetic peptides used to confirm assignments and to measure immunogenicity. Overlap between the four peptidomes was small, indicating highly individual HLA peptidomes, yet they were broadly immunogenic both in the patients the lines came from and in unrelated patients, and that cross-patient immunogenicity was only exceptionally attributable to individual peptides. Most epitopes came from low to medium abundance proteins in sensitive processes such as cell cycle control, DNA replication and tumour suppression.
  • Proteasomal spliced peptide estimation (2018): Uses de novo sequencing of HLA-I peptide spectra, filtered against UniProt and matched to spliced candidates, to estimate how many ligands are proteasome-spliced.
  • DeepNovoAA (2019): Immunopeptidome neoantigen discovery
  • Bat MHC I peptidome (2019): Crystal structures of Pteropus alecto MHC I plus de novo LC-MS/MS of peptides eluted from a random library exchange show a bat-specific three-residue insertion promotes high-affinity peptide binding.
  • DIA immunopeptidomics with PEAKS database and de novo search (2019): Combines database search and de novo sequencing on DIA immunopeptidomics data, so HLA peptides can be identified without a DDA-built spectral library.
  • CRC liver metastasis neoantigen discovery (2020): Proteogenomics and de novo sequencing of the immunopeptidomes of patient colorectal cancer liver metastases to find HLA peptides that database search misses.
  • Swine SLA-I immunopeptidome (RPLD-MS) (2021): Random peptide library plus MS de novo sequencing (RPLD-MS) defined the peptides bound by swine SLA-I variants, linked to crystal structures of the binding pockets.
  • sORF noncanonical MHC-I peptides (2021): Combines Ribo-seq-derived sORF databases with database search and de novo sequencing to identify 308 noncanonical MHC class I peptides from two tumor cell lines.
  • Unsupervised HLA-I peptidome mining with SMSNet (2021): Applies the SMSNet hybrid de novo approach to mono-allelic HLA-I peptidomics data to find new ligands, refine binding motifs and flag likely contaminant peptides in community databases.
  • Proteasome-spliced HLA ligand discovery (2022): De novo sequencing of HLA class I ligands from a colorectal cancer line found genome-untemplated proteasome-spliced peptides that CD8+ T cells recognise.
  • DeepNovo Peptidome (2022): Immunopeptidomics pipeline
  • MARS (2022): Non-canonical antigen selection
  • Noncanonical MAP atlas (2023): A proteogenomic pipeline based on deep-learning de novo sequencing plus open search mapped 8,601 noncanonical MHC class I peptides from noncoding regions across 11 cancer types.
  • pXg (2024): MHC-I noncanonical peptides
  • Neoantigen proteomics survey (2025): Surveys database search and deep learning de novo sequencing for cancer neoantigen discovery and compares tools on HepG2 and HCC samples.
  • HCC non-canonical neoantigen discovery (2026): Finds non-canonical neoantigens in hepatocellular carcinoma by mass spectrometry-based de novo sequencing and then tests them, addressing the gap that de novo proposes cryptic antigens whose immunogenicity goes unverified.
  • TIPs (2026): Deep-learning-guided proteogenomic framework that uses de novo sequencing to refine the search space for transposable-element-derived HLA antigens, finding 20-fold more than conventional searches.
  • Thai RCC personalised immunopeptidomics (2026): Profiles the personalised immunopeptidomes of 13 Thai renal cell carcinoma patients, whose HLA allotypes are underrepresented in public reference data. Builds a personalised proteome from whole-exome sequencing and searches it alongside de novo peptide sequencing, so de novo produced part of the result.

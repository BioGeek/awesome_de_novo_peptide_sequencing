Versions

full corpus

Raw MSDT files for every run, a metadata file, and global FDR-filtered FragPipe and Sage results. Deposited as PRIDE PXD071590, which was still private on 2026-10-15: the Nature Methods data statement gives reviewer access only. Assembled from 647 public projects (601 PRIDE, 46 iProX), listed in the paper’s Supplementary Table 1 and recorded here as provenance, plus 3,379 in-house raw files (Mus musculus, Homo sapiens) with no accession of their own.

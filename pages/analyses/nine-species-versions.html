“Evaluated on nine-species” names at least eleven different sets of spectra. This page puts every public copy of the benchmark on one key, (species, raw file, scan), and follows each spectrum from one version to the next: whether it stays, which split it lands in, whether its label changes, and whether the spectrum itself does. It was computed on 2026-10-20 from the files as published that day.

Every number is a link. It leads to an entry in Reproducing every number at the end of the page, which names the public files it reads, the scripts that produce its input, and the code that counts it. The whole analysis reruns from those files with one script, run_all.sh. The catalog’s own record of the versions is the nine-species benchmark’s page.

Naming. Papers call DeepNovo’s 2017 release “9-species-V1” and the re-curated benchmark “9-species-V2”, usually without saying which file. Here v1 is the 2017 release and v2 main and v2 balanced are the two variants Wen & Noble published in 10.5281/zenodo.13685813. The three dated folders of MSV000090982 are earlier builds of v2, and none of them is v2 main.

Four things to know before quoting a nine-species number.

  1. MSV000090982 is not v2 main. It holds three builds, from 2022, 2023 and 2024, and even the 2024 build is 47,640 spectra away from v2 main.
  2. The InstaNovo split, which NovoBench trains on, overlaps the newer test sets. 17.0% of ProteoBench’s spectra, 21.8% of dIon’s test set and 21.8% of denovo_benchmarks’ nine-species sets are scans from its training split.
  3. One v2 spectrum in six has a precursor on an isotope peak (17.1% of v2 main). A model that enforces the precursor mass with no isotope allowance cannot get those right; on v1 and on the InstaNovo split that never happens.
  4. v1 writes every leucine as I (0 of its labels contain an L). Fold I and L before comparing labels across versions.

What changed in the spectra that stayed

v1 was exported by PEAKS in 2017 and v2 by ThermoRawFileParser in 2022, and the two disagree about the spectrum itself.

The precursor m/z. Among the 801,288 spectra v1 shares with the 2022 build, 102,812 have a neutral precursor mass 1 Da heavier in the revised version and 17,634 2 Da heavier. Every precursor in v1 sits on its label’s monoisotopic peak (0 on an isotope peak); ThermoRawFileParser writes the m/z the instrument selected, which is often an isotope peak. The revised search “allowed for 1 isotope error in the selection of candidate peptides” (Wen & Noble, Methods), and the released MGFs keep the instrument’s m/z.

The peaks. v1’s peak lists are sparser (median 142 peaks against 334) and reach fragment m/z values no fragment of the precursor can have (up to 47,568, against 5,496). For mouse, where both sides could be downloaded whole, 19,993 spectra were compared peak by peak: a median 97.6% of v1’s peaks are in the revised peak list within 10 ppm, carrying 97.0% of its intensity, and v1’s intensities are about 2.5 times the revised ones. v1 is the same scan after PEAKS’s own processing.

Everything else barely moves: 20 shared spectra change charge, the revised builds hold no charge-1 spectra (v1 has 0.24%) and no peptide shorter than 6 or longer than 50 residues.

The modification vocabulary. v1 and the 2022 build carry oxidation, N and Q deamidation and carbamidomethylation. denovo_benchmarks’ nine species

The living benchmark’s datasets file calls its nine-species sets “De novo nine-species benchmark (MSV000090982)”. They are not. Its own deposit, MSV000096182, holds 101 raw files relabelled by its own search: 946,155 labels, keyed as <raw file>_<chunk>:<index> into MGF chunks of at most 20,000 charged spectra.

  • A different subset of files. Mouse takes all 13 raw files, including the ones v2 main drops; B. subtilis takes 4 of 106 and yeast 3 of 27; and honeybee includes BM-2, the file the revised search never had.
  • Different labels. Of the 477,999 spectra it shares with v2 main, 87.9% carry the same label; 467,142 are not in v2 main at all. 188,089 labels carry a modification the revised search did not allow: N-terminal, D, E and H carbamidomethylation, sodium on D and E, lysine and arginine methylation, and +12 and +26 N-terminal adducts.
  • A deamidation where the others see an isotope peak. On 44,464 spectra that v2 main labels at the M+1 peak, denovo_benchmarks writes the same backbone with one more deamidation in 39,011 cases. The carbon-13 reading fits better for 37,982 of them (median error 0.9 ppm against 12.1 ppm), so these labels most likely carry a deamidation that is not there.
  • A deposit that does not match its labels in two files. 1,014 labels point past the end of their MGF chunk (BC-2_1 holds 18,996 spectra and is cited up to index 19,999), so those chunks are not the files the labels were made from.

Splits, and what a model has already seen

v1’s folds are disjoint on the modified peptide, not on the backbone. No validation or test label of a fold is a training label of that fold, but 28% to 36% of every fold’s validation and test spectra carry a backbone it trains on, in another modification state. Across species, the held-out species’ spectra whose backbone the fold trained on run from 0.4% (M. mazei) to 22.6% (V. mungo). v2 was built to remove that, and did: 0 backbones of v2 main are in two species.

The InstaNovo split is v1’s yeast fold, the same training spectra NovoBench retrains on, in v1’s own order: row by row, 639,286 of its 639,286 records have the precursor and peak count of the v1 record in the same position of the yeast-excluded fold’s train and valid files, and, for its test set, of v1’s whole yeast file. Training on it and testing on another version outside yeast tests partly on training data, because the spectra are the same physical scans, re-labelled.

What to write when reporting a number

  • Name the build, not the accession. “9-species-V2” is three builds under one MassIVE accession plus v2 main and v2 balanced on Zenodo; InstaNovo’s ninespecies_updated is the 2023 build; denovo_benchmarks is a selection of its own.
  • Do not train on the InstaNovo or NovoBench split and test on a newer version outside yeast. Up to 21.8% of the test spectra are training scans, and about 64.2% carry a backbone the model trained on.
  • Allow a one-dalton precursor offset, or score the v2 spectra whose precursor is an isotope peak separately.
  • Fold I and L before comparing labels across versions.

Reproducing this page

Everything above was computed from the public files the versions table links, by the scripts in analysis/nine_species/. To rerun it:

git clone https://github.com/BioGeek/awesome_de_novo_peptide_sequencing
mkdir -p ~/data/ninespecies
cp -r awesome_de_novo_peptide_sequencing/analysis/nine_species ~/data/ninespecies/scripts
sh ~/data/ninespecies/scripts/run_all.sh                       # about 110 GB, 30 GB of RAM, a day
~/data/ninespecies/.venv/bin/python ~/data/ninespecies/scripts/page_numbers.py --check

run_all.sh lists the steps in order: list the three MassIVE deposits, fetch the HTTP sources (sources.py names every one), extract one table per source file, key every version on (species, raw file, scan), run the comparisons, and finally page_numbers.py, which computes every number on this page. Its --check compares the result with the committed page_numbers.json, which is what this page prints. The charts read site_data.json, written by export_site_data.py in the same run.

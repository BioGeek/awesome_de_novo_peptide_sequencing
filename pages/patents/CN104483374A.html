Abstract

Translated from Chinese 本发明提供了一种用MALDI-TOT-TOF(基质辅助激光解吸电离-串联飞行时间)质谱对蛋白质N端序列进行从头测序的方法和试剂盒。该方法涉及蛋白质的分离、还原烷基化、蛋白质的化学修饰、酶切肽段的质谱分析、蛋白质N端肽段的质谱识别与从头测序。该方法对胶内蛋白质的赖氨酸进行胍基化，选择性地将TMPP-Ac修饰在蛋白质N端氨基上；并通过加入终止试剂和洗涤。该方法所得的蛋白质N端氨基修饰物不受过量的试剂和相应的副产物的影响，与质谱技术相容。该方法可对蛋白质的N端肽进行富集，结合反相液相色谱和生物质谱技术，既容易确定蛋白质的N端肽段，又可以很方便地进行从头测序。 The invention provides a method and a kit for de novo sequencing of protein N-terminal sequences by using MALDI-TOT-TOF (matrix-assisted laser desorption ionization-tandem time-of-flight) mass spectrometry. The method involves separation of proteins, reductive alkylation, chemical modification of proteins, mass spectrometry analysis of enzyme-cut peptides, mass spectrometry identification and de novo sequencing of protein N-terminal peptides. The method guanidinates the lysine of the protein in the gel, and selectively modifies TMPP-Ac on the N-terminal amino group of the protein; and by adding a termination reagent and washing. The protein N-terminal amino modification obtained by the method is not affected by excess reagents and corresponding by-products, and is compatible with mass spectrometry. This method can enrich the N-terminal peptides of proteins, combined with reversed-phase liquid chromatography and biological mass spectrometry, it is not only easy to determine the N-terminal peptides of proteins, but also convenient for de novo sequencing.

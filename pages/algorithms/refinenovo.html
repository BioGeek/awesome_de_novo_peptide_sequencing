Reported comparisons (4)

The comparison tables this method’s own papers print, standardised: every value on a 0-1 scale, methods down the side, species and metric across. These are numbers papers report about themselves and their baselines. They are not a leaderboard, and they do not compare across tables: each was produced by a different group, on the dataset named in its corner, with each baseline either retrained, run from released weights or quoted from another paper. The basis column says which where the paper states it, and in most papers it does not. Bold is the best value in a column and underline the runner-up, our ranking rather than the paper’s own marks.

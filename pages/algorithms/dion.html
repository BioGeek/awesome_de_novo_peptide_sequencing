dIon: algorithm · Transformer (AR). Self-supervised DINO-style pretraining of a spectrum encoder, from a precursor-conditioned spectrum mixture and a partial spectrum with the precursor withheld…

Self-supervised DINO-style pretraining of a spectrum encoder, from a precursor-conditioned spectrum mixture and a partial spectrum with the precursor withheld; initialising a Casanovo-style de novo sequencer from it raises peptide precision over training from scratch.
